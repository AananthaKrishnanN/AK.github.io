Shear Coaxial Flow Interaction with Transverse Pressure Waves

APSYS Lab, IIT Kharagpur | Prof. Amardip Ghosh

AIAA SciTech 2027 abstract

Read AIAA SciTech 2027 Abstract ↗

Investigating how transverse pressure waves perturb density-stratified coaxial flow and generate instability and baroclinic vorticity relevant to combustion dynamics in liquid rocket injectors.

Schematic of transverse pressure interaction with coaxial flow

Designed the experimental rig and developed an end-to-end CFD framework in ANSYS Fluent, including geometry, meshing, and solver setup.

Conducted transient simulations of shock–interface interactions to investigate Richtmyer–Meshkov dynamics and baroclinic vorticity generation

Numerical Schlieren and helium mass-fraction evolution capturing the development of flow instabilities.
